About ZeroDayZ

A simple way for the hostel to hear about problems and to fix them, with everyone able to see what is happening.

How it works

  1. 1 {{ svg('pen') }}

    Register

    A student describes the problem, says where it is and can add a photo.

  2. 2 {{ svg('person') }}

    Assigned

    The complaint goes to the right staff member, who accepts it and starts the work.

  3. 3 {{ svg('check') }}

    Fixed

    The staff member marks it done, and the student says whether it is really solved.

Common questions

What can be reported?

Anything about the hostel that needs fixing: plumbing (leaks, taps, drainage), electrical (fans, lights, switches), maintenance (doors, windows, furniture) and other problems such as pests.

Where do I see progress?

Every complaint shows one of four states: waiting, in progress, fixed or not accepted. Students find them under Registered complaints.

Who can see my phone number and photo?

Only the staff and admins who handle your complaint. They are used to reach you and to understand the problem.

What if it is an emergency?

Do not wait for a complaint to be picked up. Call security straight away from the Contact page.
